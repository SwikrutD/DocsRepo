SwidScanner

A document scanner for Android. No account, no ads and no cloud processing. Pages are detected, cropped, cleaned up and read entirely on your phone.

Privacy policy · Terms of service

Features

  • Live page detection and auto-capture, or manual shutter
  • Single and batch scanning, plus book mode for spreads
  • Five filters, corner adjustment and page tools
  • Offline text recognition (OCR)
  • Export to PDF, JPEG or text, with optional password and searchable text layer
  • Save to a phone or SD card folder, or to Google Drive (optional)
  • QR and barcode scanner

Install

SwidScanner is free on Google Play. Requires Android 8.0 or later.
